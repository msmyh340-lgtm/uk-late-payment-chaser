Am I being petty for asking?

No. You did the work. Chasing payment is not rude, it is business. The polite letter is written to protect the relationship.

Can I really charge interest?

Yes. Under the Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act 1998 you can charge 8% plus Bank of England base rate, plus fixed compensation, unless your contract says otherwise.

What if they still do not pay?

The Letter Before Action gives them 14 days. After that, you can issue a claim in the Small Claims Court (Money Claims Online) and claim interest, compensation and court fees.

Is my data saved?
